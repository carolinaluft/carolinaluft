### Hi, I'm Carolina

Neuroscientist and postdoc at the Brain Institute of Rio Grande do Sul (InsCer),
PUCRS, Brazil, and member of the Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience Lab (DCNL).

I study how early-life adversity (prenatal stress, alcohol and nicotine exposure)
shapes neuroinflammation and the gut–brain axis, combining animal models, in vitro
systems, and human cohorts with multi-omics: Oxford Nanopore metagenomics and DNA
methylation, and proteomics.

**Research interests:** neuroinflammation · prenatal adversity · gut microbiome ·
alcohol use disorder · epigenetics · multi-omics

**Tools:** Python · Oxford Nanopore (Dorado, minimap2, modkit) · SLURM (CIACD/PUCRS)
